# Product Walkthrough

## Public Reader

1. Open the map or issue list and filter by category, ward, lifecycle, or
   provenance.
2. Open a public record to inspect approved evidence, source context, current
   lifecycle, handoff history, and timeline.
3. Open the proof view to compare the public version with its recorded Solana
   commitment.
4. Use a valid signal invitation to add or retract an attention signal without
   changing the issue lifecycle.

## Reporter

1. Open an intake invitation and review its scope and expiry.
2. Upload one clear image and provide the observed condition, date, category,
   and approximate location.
3. Submit for review and retain the private tracking capability.
4. Follow the private status page for review, requested changes, approval,
   rejection, withdrawal, or expiry.

Submission never creates an immediately public issue.

## Operator

1. Sign in with managed identity and complete AAL2.
2. Select an active organization and open its moderation queue.
3. Review provenance, private evidence, location safety, and the proposed
   public copy.
4. Create and approve a distinct redacted derivative, then approve or reject the
   submission with an immutable decision.
5. For published issues, append lifecycle, correction, handoff, or removal
   events through role-scoped actions.
6. Use diagnostics and reconciliation evidence to investigate blocked outbox or
   proof state without exposing internals publicly.
